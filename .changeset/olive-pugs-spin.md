Deprecated `.rvt-loader` in favor of `<rvt-loader>`, with size modifiers replaced by a `size` attribute. Also deprecated `.rvt-loader--reverse`, since color themes now adapt the loader to its context, and `.rvt-button--loading`, whose styles were dropped in Rivet 3; use `aria-busy` and `disabled` on the button instead.
