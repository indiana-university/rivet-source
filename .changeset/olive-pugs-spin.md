Deprecated `.rvt-loader` in favor of `<rvt-loader>`, with size modifiers replaced by a `size` attribute and `.rvt-loader--reverse` replaced by `type="reverse"`. Also deprecated `.rvt-button--loading`, whose styles were dropped in the Rivet 3 refactor; use `aria-busy` and `disabled` to convey a button's loading state.
